About me

I’m a Senior Drupal Front-End Developer at Gai Technology in Dharamshala, working across Drupal 8, 9, 10 and 11 as well as WordPress.

I build custom themes from scratch and lead upgrades from Drupal 8 to 9, 10 and 11, with performance and accessibility at the centre of every release. I’m at my best where design meets Drupal’s content model: Views, Layout Builder, Paragraphs and custom blocks, styled with Twig, SCSS and Pattern Lab so editors get flexible layouts and visitors get a fast, consistent interface.

I’ve led front-end delivery on HEAL Security, NISO and Collaboration, and I contribute to the Drupal community through my Drupal.org profile.
